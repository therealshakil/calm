# drift — poems (the calm words)

The few words drift sets into a cleared *ma*. This is their home — short, calm, ND-readable, UK English. The
words are Shakil's to choose; what's here is a scaffold and one placeholder, not a finished set.

Keep them: short (a haiku sits in the *ma* well — three lines, no line too long), quiet, plain, unhurried;
no `!`, nothing that makes absence feel like debt. The voice of the suite — "no rush", "this'll keep".

How drift uses them: either chosen per banner, or picked deterministically from a seed (the same seed → the
same words, alongside the same art).
